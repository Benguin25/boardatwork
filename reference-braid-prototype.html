    How to play

    Two words were braided into one string. Each word keeps its own letter order. Pull them apart.

    • Tap a letter to colour it or . Tap again to change.
    • Fill both words, then Check. You'll learn how many letters are on the wrong strand, and one of them locks into place.
    • The theme is hidden until your second check.
    • Stuck? Hint reveals the theme early, then locks a letter for you. Three hints, and they show on your result.
    • Five checks. A new braid every day.
